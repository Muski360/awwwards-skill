# Reformulation Examples

Use these reasoning patterns when a concept stays generic. Each depends on project evidence; do not borrow its art direction as a preset.

| Starting point | Project evidence and stronger direction | Mechanism, cost, and alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An empty hero gets an abstract render | An observatory has its own sky imagery and an invitation to explore. Set a clear reading/action area beside one expansive celestial subject. | Unequal visual mass and open space create tension. A still may carry the identity; use a live spatial scene only if its behavior earns the rendering cost. |
| A signup page receives animation everywhere | A generative type studio can demonstrate its craft in one expressive heading while the form stays stable. | Local pointer deformation with a legible resting state; protect text semantics and use an authored static specimen on touch or reduced motion. |
| A person-led portfolio opens with a generic claim | A performer's portrait and rehearsal material reveal different aspects of their work. Transform the portrait into a smaller editorial frame before entering the story. | Preserve a focal subject through masks, scale, and a short pause. Asset alignment and timing matter more than the library; a sequence of stills may suffice. |
| A video portfolio becomes a wall of identical cards | A filmmaker's footage supplies rhythm, color, and subject. Give one sequence prominence and use a coherent frame treatment for previews and case studies. | Alternate moving and still media so the next action stays clear. Limit concurrent decoders, use posters, and preserve mobile crops. |
| A custom 404 feels unrelated | A print archive already uses folios, crop marks, and missing-plate annotations. Extend that language into a missing-page composition. | Type and framing can create continuity at low cost. Keep a clear explanation and archive/home link; verify unknown URLs rather than only a designed demo route. |
| A product page flattens every benefit into cards | Real product workflows establish a primary decision and secondary capabilities. Give the main workflow a large annotated sequence and the rest a compact index. | Content priority controls scale and grouping. Use direct manipulation only where it helps a visitor understand the product. |
| Every section has a new effect | A music label's supplied release artwork and rhythm suggest a common transition language. Let media lead and use related timing for entrance and playback feedback. | Sequence focal attention and leave reading pauses. Preserve controls and silence until the visitor requests audio. |
| Mobile loses the desktop identity | The same story can begin with a tighter image crop, different line breaks, and a reachable sequence of chapters. | Recompose for touch; a swipeable strip may suit media, with visible controls. Keep ordinary scrolling and a complete non-gesture path. |

References can reveal a principle without proving their implementation or performance. The visual-analysis method belongs in [visual identity](visual-identity.md); engineering decisions belong in [motion and implementation](motion-and-implementation.md).
