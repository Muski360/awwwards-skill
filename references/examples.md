# Reformulation Examples

Use when a direction stays generic. These are hypothetical comparisons, not themes to assign by industry. The same brief can support different outcomes; choose through its evidence and constraints.

| Project evidence and question | Competing directions | What decides the choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A dispatch team repeatedly scans changing routes, capacity, and exceptions. How can the next decision become clear? | A dense comparison workspace versus a geographic overview with linked detail. Neither needs a promotional opening. | Test representative decisions and real data volume. Spatial relationships must justify a map; preserve readable status, keyboard access, and an equivalent list. Identity can emerge through information rhythm and precise feedback. |
| An engineered product's internal geometry explains a documented benefit. How should a visitor discover that relationship? | A manipulable exploded model versus a paced sequence of annotated sections or footage. | Prototype the revealing moment with real assets. Live 3D can offer spatial understanding or an authored atmosphere; use it when that contribution survives mobile and rendering cost. Keep essential evidence and controls in DOM, with a composed static alternative. |
| A public service serves several languages and many first-time visitors. What structure gives people confidence to act? | A task-first entrance versus a guided explanation leading into the same service. | Compare where visitors hesitate using realistic copy and constraints. Voice, type hierarchy, and navigation can establish identity without spectacle; keep eligibility and recovery clear, long translations usable, and familiar controls recognizable. |
| A cultural program supplies contrasting performances, recordings, and an evolving schedule. How can visitors explore its character and still plan a visit? | An exploratory audiovisual field versus a typographic program whose transitions connect related events. | Let actual relationships among works determine the structure. Kinetic type, shaders, spatial sound, or scene transitions are possible, not a package. Preserve readable event details, direct links, controllable movement, silence until audio is requested, and a complete touch/reduced-motion path. |

These comparisons concern structure and behavior, not just new colors or replacement imagery. Use the direction workflow in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) for the actual brief. Reference interpretation belongs in [visual identity](visual-identity.md); implementation and lifecycle safeguards belong in [motion and implementation](motion-and-implementation.md).
