# SLOP Pattern Catalog

Use when an existing treatment or your own proposal feels generic. SLOP imitates a visual fashion without a convincing relationship to the project's identity, content, hierarchy, or interaction.

These are diagnostic signals, not banned ingredients. A gradient, card, cursor, marquee, or 3D scene can be the right choice. Ask what it contributes in this project and whether a stronger treatment could do more.

## Patterns and Reformulations

| Treatment | Signal to investigate | Project-specific reformulation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gradient or blob glow | Fills emptiness without a source in the project | Derive light or color from material, imagery, state, or a purposeful atmosphere; resolve weak composition first |
| Missing identity | Safe type, neutral colors, rounded rectangles, and stock symbols could serve unrelated brands | Ground the visual thesis in the audience, work, place, product, or content |
| Glass panels | Blur decorates routine content and reduces legibility | Use depth when layers or a material metaphor matter; otherwise clarify grouping through type and space |
| Card mosaic | Every section uses equivalent containers | Let content priority determine grids, lists, tables, media, or open composition |
| Giant headline | Scale supplies the entire concept | Develop width, rhythm, line breaks, spacing, crop, and a relationship to the subject |
| Pill soup | Labels, actions, and navigation have indistinguishable emphasis | Clarify roles and interaction states; keep capsules where they fit |
| Stock abstract 3D | A floating object has no authored relationship to the project | Derive a spatial form, transformation, or atmosphere from identity; compare live rendering with a strong still |
| Effect stack | Grain, glow, parallax, cursor, marquee, and tilt demand attention together | Sequence attention through a shared motion grammar and stable reading areas |
| Information echo | Labels, icons, text, and helper copy repeat the same point | Consolidate the signal and make the next decision clear |
| Decorative density | Frames, badges, grids, and rules add noise without meaningful rhythm | Keep framing that organizes content or expresses identity; remove unrelated layers |
| Scroll staging | Input is blocked or delayed to force animation | Preserve native progress and a direct path through the narrative |
| Custom cursor | Lag, obstruction, or novelty outweighs identity and feedback | Improve the response and pointing affordances, or keep the system cursor |
| Infinite marquee | Repeated text fills a transition with no useful rhythm or meaning | Use a purposeful editorial break, concise evidence, or media sequence; keep movement controllable |
| False proof or generic copy | Invented evidence or claims could describe any company | Use real project facts, concrete language, and the brand's actual voice |
| Icon wallpaper | Identical symbols accompany every sentence | Use project-specific symbols only where they clarify meaning; let text carry the rest |
| Template section order | Hero, logo cloud, benefits, testimonial, pricing, CTA appear by reflex | Build the content spine around this audience's reading and decision path |
| Media as filler | Strong photographs or footage become interchangeable thumbnails | Compose around the subject, crop, scale, and sequence |
| Unrelated exceptional state | Loading or 404 switches to a generic visual language | Consider continuity using existing identity anchors; keep utility and recovery clear |

## Critique Your Own Work

Compare changed views and interactions with the baseline. Identify the weakest moment and ask whether you introduced a generic substitution, redundant animation, repeated composition, or an effect that competes with content. Inspect rendered evidence before declaring improvement; source-only conclusions remain provisional.

Use logo substitution as a diagnostic: investigate when the rationale and treatment fit an unrelated brand equally well and weaken this project's intended meaning. Preserve familiar task conventions and intentional restraint. A missing expressive opportunity can deserve refinement even when the page is usable.

Choose dispositions and concrete alternatives using the [audit guide](critical-design-audit.md). Scale severity to observed impact: a broken core task is a blocker; a weak signature moment may be high priority for a campaign and minor for a utility page. Do not assign severity by ingredient alone.
