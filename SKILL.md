# Awwwards Skill

Create exceptional websites by researching, interpreting, and composing around the project's purpose, audience, and material. There is no default Awwwards aesthetic. Expressive and restrained directions are equally available; choose what makes this project convincing and memorable.

User instructions and documented project constraints take priority. Preserve verified facts, authorized scope, successful existing decisions, and the working stack. Accessibility, responsive composition, and performance belong to the creative work.

## Choose the Mode

- **Build or redesign:** research, choose a direction, implement, and verify.
- **Concept:** develop the direction and feasibility; implement only when requested.
- **Audit:** inspect and prioritize evidence; edit only when fixes are authorized.
- **Narrow change:** work within the existing system and requested concern.

## Work From Evidence

Inspect the code, routes, real copy, assets, brand rules, and supplied references relevant to the task. Open the media itself when tools permit. Establish the visitor's job, desired feeling, useful action, existing language, and constraints on stack, devices, rights, and delivery. Separate observation, inference, and unknowns. Ask only for missing information that materially changes the work; keep notes proportional to the task.

## Develop and Build

1. **Establish the baseline.** Inspect affected routes and states, including desktop and mobile where possible. For new work, assess the brief and available material instead of inventing an existing experience. Protect strengths and address failures in core tasks, accessibility, factual content, and loading.
2. **Research before choosing.** For a new visual direction or substantial visual redesign, use [external research](references/external-references.md) before committing. Inspect supplied references and investigate relevant sources beyond the catalogue. Reading a guide or listing familiar links is not research. A narrow fix or an audit without a new direction does not need this detour. If research is prohibited or tools/sources are unavailable, state the evidence gap and proceed provisionally from available material.
3. **Compare and choose.** Use [visual identity](references/visual-identity.md) to connect project evidence to composition, typography, media, and behavior. When the brief leaves the direction open, challenge the initial approach with a materially different one; color or object swaps alone do not test the choice. Honor an explicitly requested direction. Choose by audience, content, identity, and feasibility; explain the decisive tradeoff briefly and continue authorized work without requiring a presentation or approval ceremony.
4. **Implement the experience.** Build a visible semantic core and prototype consequential uncertainties early with real content. Develop memorable moments across the journey: typography, image, sound, space, motion, or interaction can carry the idea. Atmosphere and delight are valid benefits. Compare native capabilities, installed dependencies, and established libraries; prefer a simpler implementation when the experience is equivalent. Finish the routes and states affected by the authorized scope.
5. **Inspect and refine.** Review the rendered sequence and its responsive compositions, rather than accepting a successful build as proof of quality. Revisit choices that weaken identity, comprehension, interaction, or finish. Use the audit and evaluation guides when their depth is warranted.

## Quality Contract

- Use strong real material before replacing it. Do not invent claims, history, clients, metrics, testimonials, or product behavior. Label permitted placeholders; generated media must not impersonate factual evidence.
- Preserve semantics, accessible names and states, focus order and visibility, contrast, readable reflow, and usable controls.
- Keep core content and actions available without hover, animation, or WebGL. Respect reduced motion, provide required media controls, and recover from failed fonts, assets, scripts, hydration, or enhancement setup.
- Preserve expected navigation and input. Custom scrolling, cursors, and spatial navigation need usable keyboard/touch alternatives and must not trap the visitor.
- Recompose small and intermediate layouts around the same identity. Account for relevant loading, empty, error, and recovery states within scope; bespoke treatments are optional.

## Verify and Deliver

Run relevant repository checks and inspect affected routes at narrow, intermediate, and wide widths. Exercise applicable keyboard/touch input, zoom, reduced motion, enhancement failures, and console errors. Measure production-equivalent loading and rendering cost when consequential media or interaction work warrants it.

Report the outcome, decisive sources and adaptations, changed files, checks actually run, and remaining gaps. Distinguish rendered evidence from source inspection and measured results from estimates. Never claim unperformed checks or award-level quality from a build alone.
